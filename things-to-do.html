Things to do

Many famous places are close together. Pick a place and explore!

Westminster Walk

Westminster Walk

This is a great walk for your first day. Start at Westminster Abbey. It is a very old church. Then stroll past the Houses of Parliament and look at Big Ben, the famous clock. Next, go to the London Eye, the giant wheel. Enjoy the view from the top! Then wander along the river.

Landmarks on the walk

  • Westminster Abbey
  • The Houses of Parliament
  • Big Ben
  • The London Eye
Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace & the Changing of the Guard

Watch the Changing of the Guard. It is a ceremony. The Royal Guards play music and march in front of the palace. They wear red uniforms and tall black hats. Come early to get a good place!

River Thames

Cruise on the Thames

Take a boat trip on the River Thames. Admire London's landmarks from the water. You can see Tower Bridge, the London Eye and the Houses of Parliament.

London Zoo

London Zoo

Discover animals from all over the world. Visit the penguins. Children love them!

Madame Tussauds

Madame Tussauds

Meet wax figures of kings, queens, film stars and sports champions. Take a selfie with your favourite star. It looks real, but it is made of wax!
