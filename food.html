Food & drink

Taste English classics. English food is better than you think!

P
Paddington's tip

Do you like marmalade? I love marmalade sandwiches! Try fish and chips too.

Fish and chips

Fish and chips

Fried fish with thick chips. People put salt and vinegar on it. Eat it outside, from the paper!

Pub lunch

The pub lunch

A classic English lunch in a traditional pub. On Sundays, try a Sunday roast. It has meat, roast potatoes, vegetables, Yorkshire pudding and gravy.

Afternoon tea

Afternoon tea

A pot of tea with sandwiches, scones, jam, cream and small cakes. Do you love sweet food? This is for you!

Street food

Street food

Visit Camden Market to taste food from all over the world: burgers, noodles, crêpes and more.
