Culture & shopping

Theatre, art, science, films and markets. There is always something to do!

P
Paddington's tip

Tip: The National Gallery and the Science Museum are free. Save your pocket money for souvenirs!

Shakespeare's Globe

Shakespeare's Globe

This is a copy of the old theatre from Shakespeare's time. It is not a modern theatre. Visit the building or watch a play, like people did 400 years ago.

West End theatres

The West End

The West End is the theatre area of London. It is famous for musicals. You can see The Lion King, Wicked, Les Misérables, Matilda, Mamma Mia! and The Phantom of the Opera.

National Gallery
Free entry

The National Gallery

A place to see beautiful art. Admire paintings by Leonardo da Vinci, Van Gogh and Monet. You do not pay for the main collection. It is free.

Science Museum
Free entry

The Science Museum

A museum for people who like modern inventions. Explore rockets, robots and old machines. You can touch and play!

Harry Potter Studio Tour

The Harry Potter Studio Tour

Explore real sets and costumes from the Harry Potter films. Walk into the Great Hall and see a small model of Hogwarts. Taste butterbeer! The studios are near London.

Camden Market

Camden Market

Wander between colourful stalls. Buy souvenirs, clothes and street food from all over the world.

Portobello Road Market

Portobello Road Market, Notting Hill

A place to shop for old objects. Buy antiques and old clothes. You can also buy souvenirs here. Do you love colourful houses? Notting Hill has a lot!
